# Question

What are the phases of engineering reasoning?

# Context

We go through many phases in our head to go from a vast unstructured land of information to a reasoned decision, what is the best way to represent these phases?

# Exploration

- We know that worksheets are going to encapsulate the majority of the process, thought logs are going to generate initial questions, and final artifacts have to be produced

- Currently we're looking at:
Formulation: Concerned with formulating a question to reason about
Exploration: Concerned with discovering potential solutions
Decision: Concerned with selecting between the potential solutions within the context of the problem
Crystallization: Concerned with producing a complete artifact that represents the distillation of the reasoning

- Feeling pressure from the word decision, it doesn't really capture the evaluation of potential solutions

- Talked to chatgpt, seems it likes this:
Formulation: Concerned with defining the engineering question
Exploration: Concerned with building understanding of the question and its possible answers
Convergence: Concerned with resolving uncertainty and arriving at a coherent conclusion
Crystallization: Concerned with producing a durable artifact that captures the outcome of the reasoning process

- I like the above, it's slightly changed initial definitions and introduced the word convergence instead of decision, which I very much like, it captures that we've created all these potential solutions, and we're now in a stage where we 'converge' to a single solution, which we then crystallize into a durable artifact

- I think that will work for now, we could potentially extract the decision phase from convergence, but this will do for now

# Decision

We will use the following classification to define the phases of engineering reasoning:

Formulation: Concerned with defining the engineering question
Exploration: Concerned with building understanding of the question and its possible answers
Convergence: Concerned with resolving uncertainty and arriving at a coherent conclusion
Crystallization: Concerned with producing a durable artifact that captures the outcome of the reasoning process
