# Question

What makes engineering information authoritative?

# Context

We have our information types classification, and we have our information authority mapping, but if feels like we don't have a clear definition of what authority is, and what types of authority there are, let's look further into this

# Exploration

- Talking with ai has led to the conclusion that we don't necessarily know what we're being authoritative over, which points to a way to define if an information type is authoritative or not, we just need to reason about whether this information type has something meaningful to be authoritative over

- Which leads to the following definition:

---

Engineering information is authoritative when it is responsible for defining a particular aspect of engineering reality. When uncertainty or disagreement arises about that aspect, the authoritative information is the source that resolves it.
